### Why it is shaped this way

- **The goldens prove the transcription is faithful, not that a formula is
  correct.** PLAN.md is explicit, and the three defects found in step 6 are the
  demonstration: all three would have survived a green golden run, because no
  golden path touches them.
- **`verified` is earned per formula, not per run.** Most of the 54 will still
  be `unverified` afterwards, and that is the status field doing its job rather
  than a gap in the tests.
- **Status changes go through the extractor.** With no authoring UI (S51), the
  catalogue is a regenerated artefact; hand-editing it is the smell CLAUDE.md
  names.
